Who operates this website

This website is published for the LEX the Husky project by [LEGAL NAME OF THE PROJECT OPERATOR] (“LEX”, “we”, “us”). For privacy questions or requests, contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

Information handled when you visit

The current website is an informational site. It does not provide visitor accounts or a contact form. The website’s hosting and security infrastructure may process technical request information, such as your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request and basic browser/device information, to deliver and protect the site. The current site build does not include a separate analytics or advertising tracker. The live burn meter makes read-only requests to PublicNode’s public Cronos RPC endpoint (using Cronos’ public RPC as a fallback) about once a minute to retrieve the $LEX total supply and the public dead-wallet balance. When the homepage opens, the recent-burn list also requests public $LEX transfer logs to the dead-wallet address. These requests contain public blockchain addresses and data, not your wallet credentials; the RPC provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address.

The Whitepaper page embeds the current source PDF from lexthehusky.com. Your browser requests that document from the official website host, which may receive normal request information such as your IP address.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files from Google’s services, which may receive technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Wallets, the blockchain and external services

Browsing this informational site does not require a wallet connection. Links may take you to third-party services such as the LEX swap, X or Discord. Those services have their own privacy practices and terms. If you use a wallet or submit a blockchain transaction on another service, your wallet address and transaction details may be public on the blockchain; blockchain records generally cannot be changed by this website.

Never share your wallet recovery phrase or private keys. This website does not ask you to provide them.

How information is used and shared

Technical request information may be used by the hosting and security providers to deliver the site, prevent abuse and troubleshoot service issues. We do not sell personal information. Hosting providers and external services may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Retention and your choices

Technical logs, if generated by the hosting provider, are retained according to that provider’s settings and retention practices. The project does not receive a visitor account profile through this informational site. You can block external font requests using browser settings, though text may display in a fallback font.

Depending on your location and the circumstances, privacy laws such as the GDPR may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact the operator using the email above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. See the European Commission’s information about data protection rights.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the website or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted here with its updated date. For privacy questions, contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].
