DAO

$LEX holders help decide the direction of the project. Vote on proposals with the $LEX in your wallet and in staking.

How it works

  1. Every $LEX you hold or have staked counts as one vote. Your power is measured at a fixed moment (the “snapshot”) before the proposal starts, so buying tokens later changes nothing.
  2. You need at least 1,000,000 $LEX (in your wallet and in staking together) at the snapshot to vote.
  3. You vote by signing a message with your wallet. It is free: no transaction is sent and no gas is paid.
  4. You can change your vote until voting closes. Only your latest vote counts.
  5. For now, only the team can create proposals. Results are advisory: the team takes them seriously and explains what it will do with them.
  6. The result is decided by For versus Against, or, when a proposal is a choice between two options, by those two options. Abstain means “I take part but choose no side”: it only counts towards the quorum, the minimum total of $LEX that must take part for a result to count.
  7. For a result to count, at least 60,000,000 $LEX and at least 10 different wallets must take part.

Proposals & voting

The next roadmap phase must be put to the community before it starts. Read the proposal outline on our roadmap. Current proposals and wallet voting remain on the official DAO.
