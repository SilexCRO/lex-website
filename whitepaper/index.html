01 · A real dog, a real pack

Built around loyalty, not hype.

LEX began with the real Siberian Husky Lex and a simple idea: a meme project should be transparent about its token, its team and what is live versus still planned. The project was founded by Bjorn Daems (B54) and launched on Cronos on 29 October 2024.

02 · Token basics

One token. Clear terms.

$LEX has a stated fixed supply of 6 billion tokens. The token contract itself has a 0% buy and sell tax. That applies wherever $LEX trades; the official website swap may separately offer an optional ecosystem-support fee.

03 · Deflation & development

Burns you can verify.

The whitepaper describes manual burns from the project burn wallet and a separate trading-and-burn bot concept. Under the current proposal draft, further bot development is one of five steps awaiting internal review and a DAO vote. The bot is not described here as live; confirmed burns can be checked on Cronos.

The source whitepaper describes a proposed bot-profit split of 25% back to trading capital, 25% to the funding wallet and 50% for $LEX buy-and-burn. Any final implementation remains subject to the published proposal and its approval.

04 · Holder features

Staking is live; rewards can change.

$LEX staking and OG Pass staking are listed as live. Reward rates and pool balances can change as pools are funded and used. Check the live staking interface for current terms before participating; this page does not promise a particular APR or return.

05 · Website swap

Optional support fee, separate from token tax.

The project whitepaper describes an optional 5% ecosystem-support fee on the official website swap only. This is separate from the $LEX contract’s 0% buy/sell tax and does not apply to $LEX trades on other exchanges. Review the swap screen before confirming a transaction.

06 · Plans move through stages

Live, proposed, or still planned.

The current roadmap keeps live ecosystem features separate from the proposed next phase. That five-step phase is still a draft for internal review; it must go to a community vote before work begins. No dates or results are promised.
