How to join the farm

Get LEX/WCRO LP

Add LEX and WCRO liquidity to the Obsidian pool. The farm accepts the LP token from that pool.

Open Obsidian →

Approve and stake

Connect on Cronos, enter your LP amount and approve it. Then stake the LP to start earning a share of the reward budget.

Withdraw and claim

Withdraw your LP whenever you choose. Claim earned $LEX during or after the campaign. Cronos network fees apply.

LP value changes with the pool price and can fall below the value of holding the two tokens separately.
