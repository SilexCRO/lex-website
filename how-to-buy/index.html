Keep your keys to yourself.

The team will never ask for a seed phrase or private key.

01

A home for your tokens.

Use a self-custody wallet that supports Cronos EVM. Install it from the wallet provider’s official website or app store listing, and follow its setup instructions.

Store recovery details securely. Never type them into this website or send them to someone offering help.

Network: Cronos EVM · Chain ID: 25
02

A little CRO for the journey.

You’ll need CRO to trade and to pay network fees. You can buy it through a supported provider or transfer it from an exchange.

Buying directly in a wallet

If your wallet offers a purchase service, check that it delivers CRO on Cronos EVM. Available providers and payment methods vary by country.

Sending CRO from an exchange

Choose the Cronos EVM withdrawal network and check the receiving address. A small test transfer can help you confirm the route before sending more.

Keep some CRO in your wallet for fees. CRO on a different network may need bridging first.
